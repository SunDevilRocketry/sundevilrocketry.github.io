**Team Leads:** Cole Diefenderfer, Melanie Huffman, and Mathias Maineri

The Sun Devil Rocketry intro team program is designed for students that are new to high-power rocketry or engineering in general. It is designed to teach the basics of rocket design and construction, including:

- CAD
- Composites Manufacturing
- Simulation
- Recovery

It's also a great place to learn to work in a team. Each intro team will do one launch per semester. The first semester is a more basic high-power project with close guidance from the team lead. The second semester is more self-guided and the project itself is more advanced. It also requires each team to prepare and present a preliminary design review (PDR) to SDR's leadership team and alumni.

Intro teams are the preferred gateway into our Advanced High Power Rocketry team.
