**Solids Team Lead:** Krushna Varpe

Sun Devil Rocketry's solid propulsion team builds experimental solid rocket motors using a custom propellant formulation. The solid propulsion team formulates, manufactures, fires, and flies custom Ammonium Perchlorate Composite Propellant (APCP) completely in house. To date, the team has fired over 40 motors, and our propellant has gone as fast as Mach 4.96! This year, the team plans to fire close to a dozen motors, scaling up to new record of 2500 lbf thrust!
