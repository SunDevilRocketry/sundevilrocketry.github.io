**Liquids Leadership Team:** Lucas Bradley (Propulsion), Joseph Quam (Propulsion), Gibson McGee (Rocket Design)

Sun Devil Rocketry's roots are as a student-led propulsion research initiative. Those roots live on in our Liquid Propulsion Research team, which is the first group at ASU to successfully design, build, and fire a liquid rocket engine.

![2025 Hotfire](/img/liquids/hot-fire.mp4)

Building on this success, the liquids team is now designing the systems needed to strap a liquid engine to the bottom of a rocket and launch it. The project is also receiving an updated data acquisition and control system designed by our Avionics team.
