**Responsible Engineer:** Nicholas Armistead (Rev. 2), Eli Sells (Rev. 3)  
**Team:** Avionics Firmware (led by Nicholas Armistead)

**Features:**
- High-Speed, High-Fidelity Data Logging on a small package
- Active Roll Control (on version 2.5.0)
- Wireless Telemetry (since version 2.6.0, transmit-only for live flight data)
- Parachute Deployment (since version 2.7.0)

The Flight Computer is the flagship project of our Avionics team. It is used primarily for flight data logging on high-power rocketry projects that need a higher rate of logging than most commercial flight computers in order to properly capture data. The club also uses it as a redundant GPS tracker via our telemetry system.

Rev. 1 (*A0002-1XX*) was designed in the summer of 2022 and was in service until the start of 2023. Rev. 2 (*A0002-2XX*) changed some of the peripherals but largely kept the design intact. This revision lasted us over two years and was used as a stable, reliable platform to develop advanced software on.

Rev. 3 entered design in late 2025 and began production in Fall 2026. The hardware team is currently building the first production units, and the firmware team is bringing up drivers for the new platform. This new platform significantly diverges from the old design and was assigned a new part number to reflect our design upgrades: *A0010-XXX*.
